# Google Play target audience and content rating

Last audited: August 11, 2026

This document records the recommended Google Play Console declarations for Cartio (`fi.cartio`). Revisit the questionnaire whenever the catalog, sharing features, advertising, external content, or other app functionality changes.

## Target audience and content

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the app contain ads? | **No** |
| Target age groups | **13–15, 16–17, and 18 and over** |
| Is the app specifically designed for children? | **No** |
| Does the store listing contain child-directed marketing? | **No** |
| Restrict minor access | **No** |

Cartio is a general shopping utility suitable for teenagers and adults. Do not select age groups below 13. Keep store graphics and descriptions focused on practical household shopping rather than children, young characters, or child-directed language.

## Store category

Use **App → Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other** for the IARC questionnaire. For the Google Play store category, use **Shopping** if it is available and accurately describes the listing; otherwise use **Productivity**.

## IARC content-rating questionnaire

Answer based on the exact wording shown in Play Console. Recommended answers for the current build are:

| Content or functionality | Answer |
| --- | --- |

| Sexuality or nudity | **No** |

| Gambling or simulated gambling | **No** |
| Fear or horror | **No** |

| Users communicating or exchanging content | **No** |
| Unrestricted internet access | **No** |

| Focus on selling age-restricted physical goods | **No** |

### Alcohol references

The bundled product catalog contains neutral grocery entries such as beer, wine, cider, and spirits. Cartio does not sell, advertise, promote, or provide instructions for alcohol.

- If the questionnaire asks whether the app **focuses on selling** alcohol, tobacco, firearms, or other age-restricted goods, answer **No**.
- If the questionnaire separately asks whether the app contains **any reference to alcohol**, answer **Yes** because those product names can appear in search results and shopping lists.
- For tobacco, illegal drugs, or drug-use references, answer **No** for the current catalog.

Accept the regional ratings calculated by IARC after submitting truthful answers. Do not manually advertise a lower rating than the certificate provides.

## Access instructions

Cartio has no login, membership, geographic restriction, or otherwise gated functionality. In Play Console's app-access section, declare that **all functionality is available without special access**.

## Release consistency checklist

1. Confirm the app still has no ads, purchases, communication, or shared user-generated content.
2. Confirm catalog changes have not introduced tobacco, illegal drugs, explicit language, or other rating-relevant material.
3. Retake the IARC questionnaire if app content or functionality changes in a way that affects an answer.
4. Keep the selected audience and store listing consistent; do not market Cartio specifically to children.
