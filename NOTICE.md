# Data attribution

Cartio's bundled offline food catalog is adapted from **Fineli Release 20**, the Finnish food composition database maintained by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL).

Copyright 2015 National Institute for Health and Welfare (THL). Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Cartio extracts Finnish and English food names, removes duplicate or deleted records, selects 3,800 distinct food entries, and maps Fineli food-use classes into Cartio's shopping categories. Fifteen generic household products maintained by Cartio are appended separately.

Source: [Fineli open data](https://fineli.fi/fineli/fi/avoin-data)
